Welcome to **Lab Notes**, the blog of the PILLAR Group at Peking University.

We plan to use this space for informal write-ups that do not fit into papers:

- **Paper deep-dives** — behind-the-scenes details, ablations, and negative results from our published work
- **Tutorials and reading notes** — on topics like large language models, reasoning, and agents
- **Lab updates** — new members, talks, and open-source releases

Stay tuned — and if you are interested in working with us, check out the [join us](/join/) page.
